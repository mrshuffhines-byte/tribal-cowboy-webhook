### 1. The Program

Tribal Cowboy will deliver one (1) "Pony Post Sprint" — a four-week classroom literacy program — to one (1) Pre-K or Kindergarten classroom at:

**School:** [SCHOOL NAME]
**Classroom / Teacher:** [TEACHER NAME]
**Expected enrollment:** Up to 18 children
**Visit schedule:** [WEEK 1 DATE], [WEEK 2 DATE], [WEEK 3 DATE], [WEEK 4 DATE]
**Visit length:** 30 minutes (Pre-K) or 45 minutes (Kindergarten) per visit

The program consists of four (4) in-classroom visits with a live miniature pony, three (3) between-visit "Pony Post" letters delivered to each student, themed take-home materials each week, and a Take-Home Parent One-Pager at the end. Full program description in the attached Program Summary (Exhibit A).

### 2. Sponsorship Fee

Sponsor will pay Tribal Cowboy **$1,750** for the Sprint described above.

Payment terms:
- 50% ($875) due upon signing this Agreement
- 50% ($875) due within 14 days of the final visit
- Payable by check, ACH, or business credit card (3% surcharge if credit card)

---

### 3. What Sponsor Receives

For the sponsorship fee, Sponsor will receive:

- **Logo placement** on the cover of each child's Pony Post Adventure Book (18 books)
- **Logo placement** on each Pony Post letter sent home (4 per child — 72 total)
- **Logo placement** on the Take-Home Parent One-Pager (18 copies)
- **Logo placement** on the photo print or digital photo provided to each family (subject to the school's photo release policy)
- **Photos from the visits** for Sponsor's own marketing use, subject to Section 5 below
- The right to publicly state that Sponsor funded the program at the named school

Logo placement is in the format provided by Sponsor: vector file (.AI, .EPS, or high-resolution .PNG), submitted to Tribal Cowboy at least 14 days before the first visit. If no logo is provided in time, Tribal Cowboy will use Sponsor's name in text form.

---

### 4. Limitations on Sponsor's Use

Sponsor's marketing rights are real but bounded. Specifically:

**4.1 — Indigenous Identity Reference.** Tribal Cowboy is owned and operated by Stacie Hines, a citizen of the Nisenan Maidu Tribe. Sponsor **may not** reference Stacie's tribal identity, Native heritage, or Indigenous ownership in Sponsor's own marketing, advertising, social media, or press materials **without prior written approval from Stacie Hines.** This includes but is not limited to phrases such as "authentic Native culture," "Indigenous traditions," "Native American heritage," "tribal," or any similar framing.

Sponsor MAY say: "We sponsored The Pony Post, a literacy program from Tribal Cowboy in Athol, Idaho."

Sponsor MAY NOT say: "We sponsored an authentic Indigenous literacy program" or similar framings that position Stacie's identity as a marketing attribute of Sponsor.

**4.2 — No Endorsement Implication.** Sponsor may not state or imply that Tribal Cowboy, Stacie Hines, the school, the teacher, or any participating child endorses Sponsor's products or services. Sponsor's logo placement is acknowledgment of sponsorship, not endorsement.

**4.3 — Tribal Cowboy Brand Usage.** Sponsor will receive a Tribal Cowboy brand usage guide on signing. Sponsor will use the Tribal Cowboy logo and program name per the guide. Sponsor may not modify, redraw, recolor, or co-brand the Tribal Cowboy logo without written approval.

**4.4 — Child Privacy.** Sponsor will receive only photos that comply with the school's photo release policy. Sponsor may not identify individual children by name in any public-facing material. Sponsor may not use photos in any context that promotes products or services aimed directly at children (e.g., sugary food, screen time, weapons) regardless of the photo's apparent suitability.

**4.5 — Quote and Story Use.** Sponsor may quote testimonials, teacher reactions, or program outcomes provided by Tribal Cowboy in writing. Sponsor may not paraphrase, embellish, or create attributions that Tribal Cowboy did not provide.

---

### 5. Photo Release and Privacy

Tribal Cowboy will collect a Photo Release form from each child's parent or guardian, per the participating school's photo release policy. Photos will be classified into three tiers based on the form:

- **Tier 1 — Public use approved:** Sponsor may use in marketing
- **Tier 2 — School internal use only:** Sponsor may not use externally
- **Tier 3 — No photography:** That child will not appear in any photo Sponsor receives

Tribal Cowboy will deliver Tier 1 photos to Sponsor within 14 days of the final visit. Tier 2 and Tier 3 photos remain with the school.

---

### 6. Cancellation, Rescheduling, and Refund

**6.1 — Visit Rescheduling.** Per the Pony Contingency Protocol (Exhibit B), any visit canceled by Tribal Cowboy will be rescheduled within 7 calendar days at no additional cost. If a visit cannot be rescheduled within 7 days for any reason on Tribal Cowboy's side, Sponsor will receive, at Sponsor's choice:

- (a) One additional visit added to the Sprint at no charge, OR
- (b) A 25% refund of the sponsorship fee (i.e., $437.50)

**6.2 — Sprint Cancellation by Tribal Cowboy.** If Tribal Cowboy cannot complete the Sprint due to circumstances within Tribal Cowboy's control (e.g., extended pony illness without backup), Sponsor will receive a pro-rated refund based on visits not delivered. Default formula: ($1,750 ÷ 4) × number of visits not delivered or made up.

**6.3 — Sprint Cancellation by Sponsor.** Sponsor may cancel this Agreement before the first visit with 30 days' written notice and receive a 50% refund of any amounts paid. After the first visit, the Agreement is non-cancelable by Sponsor.

**6.4 — School-Side Cancellation.** If the school cancels the Sprint after the first visit, the Sprint will be transferred to another school at the same grade level within 30 days, or refunded pro-rata at Sponsor's choice.

## INTERNAL NOTES — DO NOT INCLUDE IN SIGNED VERSION

**Things this agreement deliberately does NOT do:**

1. **Promise tax-deductibility.** Tribal Cowboy is an LLC, not a 501(c)(3). The sponsorship may be deductible as a marketing/advertising expense on Sponsor's books, but that determination is between Sponsor and Sponsor's accountant. Never put tax language in writing.

2. **Guarantee outcomes.** This is a literacy program, not an exam-prep product. We do not promise specific reading-score gains, standards mastery percentages, or measurable behavioral outcomes. We describe what we do; we do not guarantee what it produces.

3. **Use the word "therapy" or "therapeutic."** The program is Equine-Assisted Learning (EAL), not therapy. Idaho regulates therapy. Never bridge that line in writing.

4. **Commit to anything beyond the named school + named classroom.** One school, one classroom, one Sprint, one signed contract. If Sponsor wants to fund multiple Sprints, sign multiple agreements.

**Recommended attorney review checklist:**

- Verify all Idaho-specific statutory references
- Add an indemnification clause appropriate to Idaho law
- Add a force-majeure clause covering natural disasters, public health emergencies, etc.
- Confirm the photo release/child privacy section satisfies COPPA and Idaho student-privacy law
- Consider adding a brief arbitration or mediation clause to keep disputes out of court
