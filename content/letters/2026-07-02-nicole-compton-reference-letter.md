## Before sending — fill in these blanks

1. **Phone number** — city HR offices prefer to call; email-only reads as informal. (Letterhead and closing paragraph.)
2. **Your full last name** on the signature line — "Stacie" alone is too informal for a municipal reader.
3. **One concrete example** in the third paragraph — a real city meeting or partnership Nicole helped win. Only you know the story, and it's the single biggest upgrade this letter can get. Delete the bracketed prompt if you'd rather leave it out.
4. **Nicole's actual title/duties** — if she had a formal title or a specific secondary duty (client outreach, scheduling, vendor coordination), swap it in for "supporting the day-to-day of the business." Specific beats general.
5. **Timeline** — "the past several years" works, but exact years ("from 2023 to present") is stronger if you want to name them.
