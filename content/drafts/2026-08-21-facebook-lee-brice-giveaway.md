## Why it's built this way (algorithm notes)

- **Facebook punishes hard engagement bait** ("SHARE THIS POST!!" / "LIKE AND SHARE"). The entry steps are framed as giveaway mechanics inside a story, not commands, which is the version that survives the bait filter.
- **Comments outrank likes on FB by a wide margin.** Same tag-per-comment multiplier as Instagram.
- **The community thank-you framing travels.** FB's local audience shares things that feel like hometown news, not ads. "This one's for you" makes sharing feel like passing along good news.
- **The anti-scam line builds trust and comments.** Fake winner-DM scams hit every giveaway; saying it up front reads as credible and keeps the comment section clean.
- **Follow-up moves:**
  - Reply to comments fast in the first 2 hours. FB resurfaces posts with active back-and-forth.
  - Drop your own comment on the post Saturday ("entries are rolling in, drawing Sunday night") to bump it back into feeds without editing the post. Editing a post after publishing can reset its reach.
  - Winner announcement Sunday night as its own post, tag the winner, thank everyone, and point people at the sponsor post one more time.
- Only 2 hashtags on purpose. Hashtags do almost nothing on FB and make posts read like ads.
