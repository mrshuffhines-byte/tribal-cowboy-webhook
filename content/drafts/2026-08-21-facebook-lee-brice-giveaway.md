## Why it's built this way

- **Facebook lets the form link live in the post** — no "link in bio" dance. The link preview also makes the post bigger in feed.
- **"Tag 3" in separate comments** multiplies the comment count, which is Facebook's strongest ranking signal.
- **Form entries = email list.** Submissions arrive in Formspree tagged GIVEAWAY ENTRY, exportable as CSV with name, email, and handle.
- **Follow-up moves:** reply to comments fast the first 2 hours, bump the post Saturday with your own comment ("entries rolling in, drawing Sunday night"), winner announcement as its own post Sunday.
