## 6. Equine Activity Liability Waiver

> **WARNING:** Under Idaho law, there is no liability for an injury to or death of a participant in equine activities resulting from the inherent risks of equine activities, pursuant to Idaho Code §6-1801.

I understand and acknowledge that being around horses, ponies, draft animals, and other livestock involves inherent risks that no amount of preparation or supervision can eliminate, including but not limited to:

- Animals shifting weight, kicking, biting, bucking, rearing, or stepping on a person
- Animals spooking, bolting, or behaving unpredictably in response to sights, sounds, smells, weather, or other animals
- Falls from animals, equipment, or uneven terrain
- Injuries from tack, harness, ropes, fencing, gates, vehicles, or property hazards
- Allergic reactions to animals, hay, plants, dust, or other environmental factors
- Insect or animal bites and stings
- Weather-related risks, including sun, heat, cold, and uneven footing
- Other unforeseen circumstances inherent to working with large animals on a working property

**I voluntarily assume all risk of injury, illness, death, or property damage** to myself, any minors in my care, and any guests I bring, arising from participation in this session and from being on the property of Tribal Cowboy LLC.

**I release, waive, discharge, and hold harmless** Tribal Cowboy LLC, its owners, employees, contractors, handlers, volunteers, property owners, and affiliates from any and all liability, claims, demands, causes of action, damages, costs, and expenses arising out of or related to my participation, except in cases of gross negligence or willful misconduct.
