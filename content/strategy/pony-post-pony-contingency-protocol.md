# The Pony Post — Pony Contingency Protocol
## What Happens If the Pony Cannot Make a Visit
### Tribal Cowboy LLC — internal operations document

**Status:** Internal protocol — Stacie + any future helpers reference this when something goes wrong
**Why this exists:** The Council flagged that a live animal on a weekly schedule with a sponsor paid up is a contractual and reputational crisis without a written plan. This document is that plan. If the pony cannot make a visit — for any reason — these are the steps, in order, every time.

---

## The 5 Reasons a Visit Could Miss

1. **Pony illness** — colic, lameness, eye injury, respiratory infection, anything a vet says rules out work that day
2. **Pony injury** — kick, fence cut, foot abscess, anything that requires recovery time
3. **Transport failure** — trailer flat, truck breakdown, road closure, severe weather
4. **Stacie illness** — Stacie is the sole handler; if she cannot drive or work, the visit cannot happen
5. **School-side cancellation** — fire drill, snow day, evacuation, last-minute closure (different protocol — covered separately at the bottom)

All five share the same first principle: **the school, the teacher, and the sponsor all get the same message at the same time, in writing, before the start of the school day.**

---

## The Notification Hierarchy

Whoever you call, call in this exact order. Do not skip steps.

| Time | Action | Method |
|---|---|---|
| **6:30 AM day-of** | Call the school's main office line | Phone — leave message if no answer |
| **6:35 AM** | Text the teacher directly | Personal cell, if you have it; via the principal if not |
| **6:45 AM** | Email the principal AND the teacher AND the sponsor | Three separate emails, same content, BCC any others |
| **7:00 AM** | Confirm receipt | Reply to whoever responds first; ask them to confirm with the others |
| **By 9 AM** | Post a brief, calm update on Tribal Cowboy IG story (only if visit is publicly known) | "Today's visit to [School] is being rescheduled. The pony is resting. More soon." |

**Never** let a classroom of 18 kids find out the pony isn't coming by waiting for her at the door. That story travels fast and badly.

## The Make-Up Visit Policy

**Default policy: every missed visit is rescheduled within 7 calendar days at no additional cost to the school or the sponsor.** This is written into both the school agreement and the sponsor agreement.

Reschedule rules:
- Same teacher, same classroom, same students — never reassigned
- Same week-of-the-Sprint content — if Week 2 was missed, Week 2 is what gets delivered next
- Subsequent weeks shift only if the makeup forces it; preserve the 7-day spacing where possible
- If the Sprint must extend beyond 5 weeks total, communicate to the sponsor immediately — they need to know the calendar is shifting

If a visit cannot be rescheduled within 7 days for any reason on Tribal Cowboy's side, the Sprint gets an additional visit added at the end at no charge OR a 25% refund of the sponsor fee, sponsor's choice. **Write this into the sponsor agreement explicitly.**

## The Pony Post Letter — Keeps Going

Even when the visit is rescheduled, **the weekly Pony Post letter still gets delivered on schedule.** That is the program's continuity mechanism. If anything, a missed visit makes the letter more important — it bridges the gap and keeps the kids' anticipation alive.

If the visit is rescheduled, the letter content shifts slightly:

> Hi friends! 🐴
>
> I wasn't able to come see you on Wednesday. I am sorry. I had to rest my [hoof / tummy / breath] for a few days, but my doctor said I will be ready very soon.
>
> Stacie is going to bring me to your classroom next [day]. I will wear my [costume preview without spoiling].
>
> Until then, here's a new word for you: ***rest***. Even ponies need rest sometimes. What does rest look like for you?
>
> See you very soon.
>
> Love,
> 🐴 The Pony

## When the Pony Genuinely Cannot Continue the Sprint

If the pony has a serious injury or illness mid-Sprint that prevents her returning within 7 days, and **no backup pony is available**, two options:

**Option A — Pause and resume**
With school and sponsor agreement, pause the Sprint for up to 14 days. Resume with the same pony from where you left off. All parties must agree in writing (email confirmation is fine).

**Option B — Refund or credit**
Refund the sponsor a pro-rated amount for any visits that cannot be completed. Default formula: ($1,750 ÷ 4) × number of missed visits not made up. So if Week 4 cannot happen and the Sprint ends at three visits, refund $437.50.

**Document everything.** Vet notes. Date stamps. The communication trail with the school and sponsor. This is what protects you if a sponsor pushes back later.

---

## Backup Pony Considerations

Right now, the program runs on one pony. **That is a single point of failure.** Two options for medium-term resilience:

1. **Train a second pony** to the same calm classroom standard. Takes 60–90 days of consistent classroom-environment desensitization. Worth it before Sprint #6.
2. **Identify a "stand-in" arrangement** with another local pony owner whose animal is known calm. Pre-vet the animal once; have a written substitution agreement on file.

Until one of those is in place, **Stacie should not commit to more than 6 Sprints per year** and should leave at least 2 weeks of buffer between scheduled Sprints to allow for makeup visits.

## School-Side Cancellation (different protocol)

If the school cancels the visit (snow day, fire drill, evacuation, etc.):

1. Confirm with the principal/teacher the same morning
2. Suggest 2–3 makeup dates within 7 days
3. Lock the new date by end of day
4. Update the sponsor by email — brief, no apology needed ("Today's visit at [School] was canceled on the school's side due to [reason]. Rescheduled to [date].")
5. Letter delivery shifts to the new schedule
6. No refund or contract change — school cancellations are handled as part of normal scheduling

## What's in the Truck for Every Visit (so you're ready to pivot)

- Backup phone charger (in case you need to make the calls from the parking lot)
- Printed copy of every school contact's phone + email + the principal's direct line
- Printed Pony Post letter for the week (in case you can't deliver in person, you can drop it at the office)
- Printed Idaho §6-1801 warning placard
- Printed COI for that school district
- Vet's after-hours number
- Roadside assistance number for the truck and trailer
- $50 cash for emergencies
