Billo Pitch: Filming Day

Before you record

  • Light: face a window or stand in open shade. Sun behind the camera.
  • Camera: at eye level. Wipe the lens.
  • Frame: waist up. Eyes one third down from the top.
  • Sound: phone 2 to 3 feet away. Fans off. Animals away.
  • Clothes: solid color, no logos. Same outfit all day.
  • Direction: vertical, unless Billo's upload page says otherwise.
