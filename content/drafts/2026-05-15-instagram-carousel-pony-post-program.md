## CAPTION (final, brand-voice approved)

Reading scores move when something makes the words feel real. The Pony Post is built to do that.

The Pony Post brings a calm pony into Pre-K and Kindergarten classrooms once a week for four weeks. Each visit is themed. Each week, every kid gets a real letter in the mail from the pony with a clue about what's coming next. By the end of the Sprint, each child writes their own name, understands syllables, owns four take-home books, and has met 19 Idaho Kindergarten ELA standards.

The program is built on the National Reading Panel's 5 Pillars of Reading, Scarborough's Reading Rope, and the National Early Literacy Panel's early predictors. It is aligned to Idaho's full Communication-Language-Literacy domain in the Early Learning eGuidelines. This is the Science of Reading — delivered through something a five-year-old actually wants to pay attention to.

One of the four weeks draws on North Idaho's own history. Pip, named for the real pit ponies who worked the Silver Valley silver mines outside Kellogg, comes to class with a lesson rooted in this region. Not as decoration. As curriculum.

Cost to the school is zero.

A single sponsor funds one full 4-week Sprint — $1,750 covers everything. Your logo goes on the back cover of all four take-home books. That is 72 books, each with your logo on the back cover, leaving school and going home with a kid who used them for four weeks.

Sponsorships are one per Sprint.

If you're a principal, curriculum coordinator, or district administrator in North Idaho or Eastern Washington and want to bring The Pony Post to your building this fall, reach out at info@tribalcowboy.com or find availability at www.tribalcowboy.com.

If you're a local business that wants your name on something that matters — same address.

Stacie Hines (Nisenan Maidu) runs Tribal Cowboy LLC out of Athol, Idaho.

## POSTING RECOMMENDATION

- **Day:** Tuesday or Wednesday
- **Time:** 7:00–8:30 AM PT or 11:30 AM–1:00 PM PT
- **Why:** Principals check phones before the first bell and at lunch. Tuesday/Wednesday catches beginning-of-week planning before the week goes sideways. Avoid Monday (meeting flood) and Friday (mentally gone).
- **Boost recommendation:** $5–10/day for 7 days. Target: educators and business owners in Kootenai, Bonner, and Shoshone counties + Spokane County. Ages 30–55.

## DESIGN NOTES FOR THE CAROUSEL DESIGNER

- **Color palette:** Warm gold, deep green, cream, teal accents — pull from existing Tribal Cowboy brand
- **Fonts:** Headlines in serif (something like Lora or Playfair Display). Body in Lato or Inter for readability
- **Logo:** Small, never dominant
- **No stock photography** — every photo should be a real pony, real classroom, real envelope. If a shot doesn't exist yet, use typography-only slides instead of stock
- **Consistency:** All 7 slides should feel like one set — same font sizes, same margins, same color palette
- **Mobile-first:** Test every slide at phone size before approval. Text must be legible without zooming.
