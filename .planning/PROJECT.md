# Real Estate Outreach — Tribal Cowboy Photo Campaign

## What This Is

A ready-to-post social media campaign targeting real estate agents, brokers, team leads, and adjacent businesses (mortgage, title) in North Idaho and Eastern Washington. The campaign markets Tribal Cowboy's horse photography prop service with SpotMyPhotos instant branded photo delivery as a client appreciation event differentiator.

## Core Value

Every shared guest photo has the broker's logo on it — the campaign makes that one sentence land.
