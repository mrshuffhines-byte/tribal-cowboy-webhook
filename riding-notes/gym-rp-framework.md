## The science in plain English

RP training is built on five evidence-based pillars. Every choice in this program traces back to one of them.

### 1. Volume Landmarks (sets per muscle group per week)

| Term | What it means |
|---|---|
| **MV** (Maintenance Volume) | The minimum volume to *keep* what you have. Used in deload weeks. |
| **MEV** (Minimum Effective Volume) | The minimum to *grow*. Start a mesocycle here. |
| **MAV** (Maximum Adaptive Volume) | The sweet spot for growth. Most of the mesocycle lives here. |
| **MRV** (Maximum Recoverable Volume) | The ceiling. Above this you accumulate fatigue faster than you can recover. |

**Weekly targets** (across both gym days combined, adjusted for a returning trainee with joint sensitivity):

| Muscle group | MEV | MAV | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Glutes | 6 sets | 10-14 sets | Highest priority. The seat depends on this. |
| Hamstrings | 4 sets | 8-12 sets | RDL is your bread and butter. |
| Quads | 6 sets | 8-12 sets | Lower priority for riding, present for joint balance. |
| Hip abductors | 4 sets | 6-10 sets | Specific to your weak-and-tight pattern. |
| Core (anti-rotation/flexion) | 4 sets | 8-12 sets | Pallof, dead bug, bird dog. NOT crunches. |
| Back / posture | 4 sets | 6-10 sets | Lat pulldown, rows. |

### 2. RIR — Reps in Reserve

RIR = how many more reps you could've done before failing. **2 RIR means you stopped with 2 reps in the tank.**

Train close enough to failure to drive adaptation, but not so close every set you wreck recovery. RP progresses RIR across a mesocycle:

| Week | RIR target |
|---|---|
| 1 | 4 RIR (easy, build the pattern) |
| 2 | 3 RIR |
| 3 | 2 RIR |
| 4 | 1 RIR |
| 5 | 0-1 RIR (peak week) |
| 6 | DELOAD — 4-5 RIR, half sets, lighter weight |

### 3. Mesocycle Structure

Five weeks of progressive overload + one week of deload = one mesocycle.

12-week plan = **two mesocycles back to back:**
- Mesocycle 1: Weeks 1-6
- Mesocycle 2: Weeks 7-12

The deload is non-negotiable. Skip it and you stop progressing.

### 4. Progressive Overload

Each week, add **one of these** (priority order):
1. Add a rep on a set where you hit the top of the range with target RIR
2. Add 2.5-5 lbs to a lift where you hit the top of the range AND felt like there was more
3. Add a set on a lift if recovery feels easy and you want more volume on that muscle

Track every set. Format:
```
Goblet Squat
Set 1: 25 lbs × 10 reps @ 3 RIR
Set 2: 25 lbs × 10 reps @ 2 RIR
Set 3: 25 lbs × 9 reps @ 2 RIR
```

### 5. Exercise Selection

Dr. Mike's six criteria:
- Stable
- Allows progressive overload
- Good ROM through the target muscle
- Targets the muscle directly
- Safe to train to failure
- Low fatigue-to-stimulus ratio

Every lift in this program meets all six. Variations swapped for joint friendliness (goblet squat instead of back squat, DB RDL instead of conventional deadlift) — same stimulus, lower joint cost.

## GYM DAY A — Posterior Chain + Hip (Tuesdays)

### Warm-up (10 min)
- 5 min easy cycling
- Couch stretch 60 sec each side
- 90/90 hip switches × 10
- Glute bridge squeeze-first × 10
- Banded clamshell × 12 each side
- 1 light warm-up set of the first lift at ~50% working weight

### Main lifts (rest 90-120 sec between superset rounds)

**Superset A**
| Exercise | Sets × Reps | Why |
|---|---|---|
| A1 — Goblet Squat | 3 × 8-10 | Stable, full ROM. Goblet position prevents lower-back overload. |
| A2 — Romanian Deadlift (DBs) | 3 × 8-10 | Highest-stimulus hamstring + glute lift. DBs kinder to lower back. |

**Superset B**
| Exercise | Sets × Reps | Why |
|---|---|---|
| B1 — Hip Thrust (barbell or DB) | 3 × 8-12 | THE highest-stimulus glute lift. Top of Dr. Mike's tier list. |
| B2 — Cable Hip Abduction | 3 × 12-15 each leg | Direct glute medius. Best lift for the weak-and-tight hip pattern. |

### Stability + Core (8 min)
| Exercise | Sets × Reps | RIR |
|---|---|---|
| C1 — Dead Bug | 2 × 8 each side | 2-3 |
| C2 — Pallof Press | 2 × 10 each side | 2-3 |

### Finisher (5 min)
- Suitcase Carry — 4 walks of 30 sec, alternating sides. Both shoulders level the whole time.

### Cool-down (5 min)
Couch stretch 90 sec each side · Figure-4 60 sec each side · 2 min rib-side breathing

## GYM DAY B — Single Leg + Stability (Fridays)

### Warm-up (10 min) — same structure as Day A

### Main lifts

**Superset A**
| Exercise | Sets × Reps | Why |
|---|---|---|
| A1 — Step-Up onto 12" Box | 3 × 6-8 each leg | Mounting + lope push-off + glute. Drive through front heel — no push-off from floor leg. |
| A2 — Bulgarian Split Squat | 3 × 6-8 each leg | Single-leg gold. Calls out asymmetry mercilessly. |

**Superset B**
| Exercise | Sets × Reps | Why |
|---|---|---|
| B1 — Single-Leg Glute Bridge | 3 × 6-8 each side | Direct unilateral glute work. Hips stay level. |
| B2 — Lat Pulldown or Seated Row | 3 × 10-12 | Riding posture — shoulders back, chest open. |

### Stability + Core (8 min)
| Exercise | Sets × Reps | RIR |
|---|---|---|
| C1 — Bird Dog | 2 × 8 each side | 2-3 |
| C2 — Wall Sit with Banded Knees | 2 × 45 sec | hold to discomfort |

### Partner Finisher (5 min)
- Banded clamshell hold — 30 sec each side
- Back-to-back wall sit with husband — 45 sec × 2 rounds. **Whoever drops first buys dinner.**

### Cool-down (5 min) — same as Day A

## Form rules from Dr. Mike's exercise execution playbook

1. **Controlled eccentric (~2 sec), intentional concentric.** No slow-mo grinding. That's broscience, not RP.
2. **Full ROM.** Goblet squat to thighs parallel. Hip thrust to full lockout. Lat pulldown elbows fully down.
3. **Mind-muscle connection on isolation lifts.** Hip thrust: feel glutes, not back. Cable abduction: feel side of butt.
4. **Breathing: exhale on the effort.** No Valsalva at your loads. Post-tuck + pelvic-floor caution = no breath-holding.
5. **Stop when form breaks**, not when you hit the rep number.

## The deload week

Critical and skipped by 90% of trainees. Without it you stop progressing.

- Cut sets in half (3 → 1 or 2)
- Drop weight ~30-40%
- Stay at 4-5 RIR
- Same lifts, same days, just half the work
- Sleep more, eat the same, hydrate more
- **Feels too easy. That's the point.**

After deload: restart next mesocycle at MEV with 4 RIR.

---

## How to tell if it's working

By end of Mesocycle 1 (Week 5):
- Added at least 1-2 reps OR 5-10 lbs to most lifts
- Recovery manageable (sore 24-36 hrs, not 4 days)
- Form tighter, not sloppier
- Energy higher

If recovery is wrecking you by Week 4 — above MRV, cut a set.
If lifts feel easier and easier with no challenge — below MEV, add a set.

## Red flags (RP-aligned)

- 3+ days of soreness on same area → above MRV, cut a set
- Strength dropping week to week → above MRV
- Sleep getting worse on hard weeks → push deload up
- Pelvic floor pressure or leakage → drop weight, message PT
- Doming around tummy-tuck scar → stop that lift, change variation
- Joint pain lingering >24 hours → drop the offending lift
- Loss of appetite/motivation 3+ days → deload now
