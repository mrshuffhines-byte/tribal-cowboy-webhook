FAQ

When does it actually launch?

We're collecting the first 50 photographers now to make sure we build what the arena actually needs. Beta seats open as soon as that's hit.

How accurate is the face recognition?

It's been tested on our own events with helmets, hats, dust, and motion blur. Real-world accuracy in the arena, not lab numbers. We'll publish exact benchmarks when beta opens.

Does it work offline?

You can pre-upload galleries when you have connection. Guest-side requires connection. Offline-first mode is on the roadmap and one reason we want your feedback now.

Who owns the photos?

You do. Always. ArenaShot is a delivery tool, not a stock library. Galleries can be deleted any time.

How do guests pay?

Stripe checkout inside the gallery. Money lands in your account. We take a transaction fee, never a cut of your monthly.
