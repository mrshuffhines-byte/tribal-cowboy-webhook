## Caption (Option B — story-first)

Kids meet our ponies in person all summer. This winter I made something so they could take a pony home.

*Pony, Pony, What Do You See?* started as a way to teach colors — brown, yellow, big, small, spotted — the way little ones actually learn, by saying it back to you. By the last page there's a zebra, and that's the one they remember.

Watercolor ponies, one line per page, and a rhythm any 3-year-old will have memorized by the third read.

I wrote it. My name's on the cover. Still a little surreal.
