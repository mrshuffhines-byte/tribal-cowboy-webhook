## Caption

Where the pony writes back.

The Pony Post is a 4-week reading sprint we bring into classrooms — Pre-K through 2nd grade. A real pony shows up once a week. She helps teach the lesson. Between visits, she mails the class hand-folded letters.

20 sight words. Syllables counted with a real hoof. The last day, Abby the Clydesdale shows up — 1,800 pounds, hooves the size of dinner plates.

Free to schools. Funded by sponsors who want their name on something that actually teaches kids to read.

If you know a school in North Idaho or Eastern Washington, send them our way.
