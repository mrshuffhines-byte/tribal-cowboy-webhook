## The Sight Words for Visit 1

**5 Dolch Pre-Primer words:** *I, am, a, the, you*

**Idaho ELA standards hit:** K.RF.3.c, 1.RF.3.g (sight word fluency)

The teacher gives each kid a quick before/after check (read the list aloud to each kid privately, mark which words they recognize). This becomes the **outcome data we hand sponsors** at the end of the Sprint.

---

## Pre-Visit Setup (Teacher does this the Monday before)

The teacher reads the class a "Welcome from Daisy" note. Builds the anticipation:

> *Hi friends! My name is Daisy. I am a pony. I live with my owner Stacie at a place called Tribal Cowboy. On Wednesday I am coming to your school to meet you. I cannot wait. Please be ready to read. — Love, Daisy 🐴*

---

## Run-of-Show (45 minutes)

| Time | Activity | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| **0:00–0:05** | Arrival + introduction | Stacie introduces herself in one sentence: *"I am Stacie Hines, I am Nisenan Maidu, and this is Daisy. The land we're on today is the traditional home of the Schitsu'umsh people."* No lecture. |
| **0:05–0:15** | Pony anatomy + 4 vocab words | Point on Daisy: **hoof, mane, withers, tail.** Kids touch their own body where Daisy's body part is (mane = hair, withers = shoulders, etc.). |
| **0:15–0:30** | Sight word relay | 5 laminated cards on the floor: **I, am, a, the, you.** Each kid takes a turn walking a few steps beside Daisy (Stacie holds the lead), stops at a card, reads it aloud. Daisy "delivers" the word. |
| **0:30–0:40** | Read-aloud | One Native-authored picture book. Class sits in a semicircle, Daisy stands nearby. Recommended titles below. |
| **0:40–0:45** | Q&A + goodbye | Hand out the **Pony Passport** booklet, one per kid. Tell them Daisy will write them a letter this week. |

>
> Love,
> **Daisy 🐴**

## Stacie's Delivery Notes (for the facilitator)

- **One sentence of Indigenous land acknowledgment.** Not a paragraph. The kids absorb more from one clean sentence than from a speech.
- **The 30-second per kid in the sight word relay matters.** Don't let the line bunch up. Move the pony slowly. Every kid gets the same beat.
- **Watch the teacher's face.** If the teacher is in tears by minute 35, you have a Sprint customer for next year and a parent-network referral. Send a follow-up email that night.
- **End early.** Always close the visit 2 minutes before the bell. Leave the room wanting more.

---

## How to Tell If Visit 1 Worked

- [ ] Every student read at least one sight word out loud during the relay (or got close, with no shaming).
- [ ] Every student touched Daisy (or chose not to, calmly — that counts).
- [ ] The teacher said yes when asked if Daisy can come back next week.
- [ ] The class is talking about Daisy at pickup.

If you got all four, Visit 1 worked.
